## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet, focused discipline of a curated reading room and botanical field index. It merges literary-editorial discipline with a tactile, organic minimalism. The aesthetic rejects clinical dashboard chrome, neon saturations, and pure artificial pitch-blacks, opting instead for physical parchment warmth, deep woodland ink, and grounded emerald tones.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Intentional readers, researchers, and content collectors who prioritize focused cataloging, discovery, and thoughtful notation over rapid, dopamine-driven social feeds.
- **Atmosphere:** Unhurried, breathable, authoritative, and tactile. Interfaces evoke smooth rag paper, letterpress margins, and bespoke linen bindings.
- **Design Philosophy:** Organic minimalism layered over physical print geometry. Structural authority is communicated through hairline rules, strict typographic measures, and tactile micro-badges rather than loud elevations or heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical tones, uncoated book papers, and heritage ink pigments. Contrast is natural and warm, avoiding eye strain during long-form reading sessions.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas (`#f7f5ef`):** The primary paper floor. A warm, non-glare parchment base for all views.
- **Surface (`#fffefa`):** Elevated cards, modals, and container sheets simulating premium ivory paper.
- **Surface Muted (`#f8f9f6`):** Soft sage mist for segmented controls, recessed wells, and inactive utility states.
- **Surface Warm (`#fbfaf6`):** Delicate linen cream for header bars and subtle tonal contrast against canvas parchment.
- **Deep Ground (`#1e302d`):** Deep evergreen forest tone applied exclusively to persistent navigation rails on wide displays to ground the layout.

### Brand & Interactive
- **Primary Emerald (`#34755e`):** The primary botanical anchor, active icons, brand accents, and active focus points.
- **Primary Strong (`#2f725b`):** High-contrast interactive button fills, active tabs, and category selection.
- **Primary Deep (`#245b49`):** Pressed button states and high-contrast text placed on pale sage backgrounds.
- **Primary Soft (`#e7f1e6`):** Pale mint wash for icon containers, secondary buttons, and active selection pills.
- **Primary Highlight (`#9ad9b7`):** High-vibrancy mint used as the emblem container in the dark navigation rail.

### Typographic Inks
- **Primary Ink (`#29382f`):** Dense forest charcoal for primary titles, card headers, and high-visibility text. Never use `#000000`.
- **Deepest Ink (`#25332d`):** Reserved for hero headers and prominent display titles.
- **Woodland Slate (`#35433b`):** Readable, warm slate for continuous body paragraphs, notes, and AI on-ramps.
- **Secondary Ink (`#68766d`):** Olive gray for section descriptions, inactive tab labels, and auxiliary text.
- **Muted Gray (`#879188`):** Moss gray for metadata, timestamps, character counters, and author subtitles.
- **Quiet Slate (`#9ba59d`):** Catalog numbers, placeholder hints, and subtle colophons.

### Accents & Functional States
- **Terracotta Warm (`#d7805b`):** Punctuation dividers, bullet separators (`·`), and editorial emphasis.
- **Save / Positive (`#337e69`):** TBR (To Be Read) shelf actions and confirmation states.
- **Reading Blue (`#476da1`):** Read shelf state, media badges, and completed status.
- **Snooze Gold (`#8a6e46`):** Deferred states, return labels, and temporal tags.
- **Error / Danger (`#a9443d`):** Brick crimson for validation alerts, destructive triggers, and sync warnings.
- **Hairline Border (`#eee9df`):** Default 1px card and divider outline.
- **Stone Border (`#e5e4d9`):** Input boundaries and active track outlines.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the literary authority of **Newsreader** for primary titles and editorial focal points with the crisp, neutral legibility of **Public Sans** for metadata, interface components, and dense reading copy.

### Typographic Hierarchies
- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Used for large page intros, book titles, and sheet destinations. Letter spacing is pulled slightly inward (`-0.5px` to `-0.2px`) to mimic the optical kerning of historic book covers.
- **Prose & Body (`Public Sans`):** Neutral sans-serif geometry with open counters and generous line-height (`1.5x`) allows extended reading without visual fatigue.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Labels (`Public Sans`):** Always transformed to uppercase with aggressive letter tracking (`+0.8px` to `+1.2px`). Micro-eyebrows act as navigational indexes and categorical signposts without requiring large type scales.
- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to catalog identifiers, shelf counts, and progress trackers to prevent layout shift during updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is disciplined by an intentional 4px base increment, transitioning gracefully from single-column handheld cards to a multi-column literary index on widescreen interfaces.

### Structural Grids & Breakpoints
- **Compact Viewport (`< 700px`):** Single-column flow with `18px` (`1.125rem`) horizontal edge margins. Modals morph into anchored bottom sheets. Cards span full width between margins.
- **Medium Viewport (`700px - 819px`):** Centered layout with max card measure of `560px`. Modal presentations become floating dialogs with explicit boundary cards.
- **Wide Viewport (`>= 820px`):** Introduces a permanent `238px` Deep Forest (`#1e302d`) navigation rail on the left. The remaining viewport houses an editorial canvas capped at `1050px` outer max width, centered with `56px` (`3.5rem`) margins.

### Spacing Principles
- **Editorial Proportions:** Card internals use generous breathing space: standard discovery cards feature `24px` to `32px` padding to balance large serif typography against white margins.
- **Vertical Rythm:** Major structural sections maintain `24px` to `32px` margins; micro metadata items use tight `4px` to `8px` inline spacing.
- **Measure Bounds:** Reading text blocks are capped at `65ch` (roughly `420px` to `480px`) to preserve optical reading flow across wider screens.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast drops, dramatic blur glows, and artificial glossy elevation. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering**, **fine hairlines**, and **botanically tinted ambient shading**.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Un-elevated Warm Parchment (`#f7f5ef`). Forms the resting floor for root views.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Lists):** Ivory Paper (`#fffefa`) framed with a continuous 1px Hairline Parchment border (`#eee9df`). Elevated slightly with an ambient shadow tinted in deep woodland pine (`#13241c`): `0px 2px 8px rgba(19, 36, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Pickers & Reveal Cards):** Ivory surfaces with an ambient spread: `0px 8px 24px rgba(19, 36, 28, 0.07)`, coupled with a 1px border (`#eee9df`).
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** Solid Ivory Paper container over a Deep Forest scrim (`rgba(30, 48, 45, 0.45)`). Backed by an ambient drop: `0px -6px 32px rgba(19, 36, 28, 0.16)` on mobile sheets, and `0px 12px 36px rgba(19, 36, 28, 0.18)` on desktop dialogs.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes an intentional contrast between tactile rounded enclosures and crisp architectural cards.

### Shape Tiers
- **Structural Containers (`20px - 26px`):** Main discovery reveal cards (`22px`), hub navigation cards (`22px`), and bottom sheet headers (`26px top corners`) create welcoming, paper-like silhouettes.
- **Interactive Surfaces (`14px - 16px`):** Action buttons, input containers, category tiles, and list item rows use a balanced medium curve (`14px - 16px`) providing comfortable touch bounding.
- **Micro Tags & Action Pills (`4px` or `999px`):** Utility tags, reading badges, and status pills utilize either a firm low radius (`4px` for structural editorial labels) or full pill curvature (`999px` for category chips and interactive segmented tabs).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action CTA:** Height `50px`, border radius `14px`. Solid fill in Emerald Strong (`#2f725b`), label in Warm Cream Contrast (`#fffaf6`) using `label-md` uppercase tracked typography. Active state darkens to Deep Pine (`#245b49`). Disabled state drops to `0.45` opacity.
- **Secondary / Soft Button:** Height `44px` to `50px`, border radius `14px`. Soft Mint Wash background (`#e7f1e6`) with Primary Strong (`#2f725b`) label and matched SVG icon.
- **Micro Utility Chips:** Height `32px`, border radius `4px`. Low-radius utility chips (`goldSoft`/`goldDark` for Snooze, `brandSoft`/`brandStrong` for Return) displaying concise uppercase labels.
- **Tactile Feedback:** Scale-down to `0.98` with a 120ms timing curve on active tap/press.

### Text Inputs & Search Fields
- **Single-line Field:** Height `52px`, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e5e4d9`, corner radius `14px`, horizontal padding `14px`. Text in Primary Ink (`#29382f`), placeholder in Quiet Slate (`#9ba59d`).
- **Multiline Composer:** Minimum height `120px`, padding `14px`. Accompanied by a live character counter anchored to the bottom-right in Moss Gray (`#879188`).
- **Focused State:** Border transitions to Primary Emerald (`#34755e`) with a faint `2px` concentric ring in `rgba(52, 117, 94, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Discovery Modules
- **Discovery Split Card:** Ivory Paper (`#fffefa`) surface, `22px` border radius, `1px solid #eee9df`. On desktop, a 37/63 horizontal ratio splits the visual book cover frame from the editorial summary notes. Mobile stacks the cover vertically. Includes a dark translucent top-left badge (`rgba(27, 42, 37, 0.82)`) and tracked uppercase eyebrows (`THE SHORT TAKE`).
- **Interactive Action Triad:** Below the card, 3 equal action blocks (`Save`, `Read`, `Snooze`) feature a `38px` tinted icon container (`${actionColor}18`) paired with bold semantic labels.

### Segmented Controls & Chips
- **Segmented Control:** Recessed Sage Mist track (`#f8f9f6`), `4px` interior padding, `1px solid #e5e4d9` outline. Active pill uses Emerald Strong (`#2f725b`) with white text and a subtle `0.5px` border.
- **Filter Chips:** Height `34px`, border radius `999px`, background `#fffefa`, border `1px solid #e5e4d9`. When selected, fills with Emerald Strong (`#2f725b`) and swaps label color to `#fffaf6`.

### Checkboxes & Selectable Rows
- **List Checkbox Row:** Ivory surface, height `54px`, `14px` border radius, `1px solid #eee9df`. Features a `20px` checkbox with an emerald fill and crisp white check glyph on active selection.
- **Swatch Indicator:** List rows include an inline `4px × 40px` vertical color accent bar on the left edge denoting the entry's custom editorial theme.

### Bottom Sheets & Modals
- **Header:** Features a `42px` rounded-square (`14px` radius) icon badge in Soft Mint (`#e7f1e6`) on the left, paired with a circular `42px` close button in Sage Mist (`#f8f9f6`) on the right.
- **Corner Radii:** `26px` top-left and top-right radii on mobile sheet viewports.
